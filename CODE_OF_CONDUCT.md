# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Circle of Devkin is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Expected behavior includes thoughtful communication, constructive feedback, and respect for different experience levels and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be made privately through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
